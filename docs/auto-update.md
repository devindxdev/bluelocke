# Auto Update

Bluelocke checks for updates when you open it. Follow the message on your phone if an update is available.

## Check for an Update Yourself

1. Open Bluelocke in Scriptable.
2. Tap the information icon, or go to **Settings > Help & Support > Help & updates**.
3. If an update is available, choose **Click to Auto Install** and follow the instructions.

Keep the same script name in Scriptable so your saved settings are kept.
