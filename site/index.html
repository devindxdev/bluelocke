Bluelocke brings the most requested vehicle features natively to your iOS ecosystem, without bloated manufacturer apps.

Primary Focus

Walk-away Auto Lock

Never wonder if you left your car unlocked again. Bluelocke leverages iOS Shortcuts and Automations to automatically ensure your vehicle is locked as you walk away.

Home & Lock Widgets

Keep your vehicle's status at a glance. Beautifully crafted, native-feeling widgets.

Siri Commands

"Hey Siri, cool down the car." Deep integration with iOS Shortcuts lets you control your vehicle with just your voice.

In-App Controls

A minimal interface for when you need manual control. Instantly lock/unlock doors, trigger climate controls, or force a status refresh directly within the script.
